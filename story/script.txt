In this world, every time you laugh, you glow one shade brighter. That's why funny kids light up whole hallways, and serious people are so dim you bump into them. I was pretty normal. But my best friend Ollie was the dimmest kid alive. He'd never laughed once. Our teacher marked him absent while he stood right there. Meanwhile, Jasper was so bright, you needed sunglasses. At lunch, he sneezed milk out his nose. I laughed so hard I glowed one shade brighter. So I had an idea. If I laughed at everything, I'd be the brightest kid in school. I fake-laughed at a math test. Nothing. Then our teacher sat on a whoopee cushion. I laughed for real, and lit up like a lamp. So I hung out with funny kids, watched cat videos, read joke books. By sunset, I was almost as bright as Jasper. But then I saw Ollie, alone in a dark corner. I'd spent all day ignoring my best friend. So I went over. Want to hear the worst joke ever? Why did the cookie see a doctor? It felt crumby. Ollie snorted. Then he laughed, and for the first time, he glowed. He laughed until he was brighter than me. Next morning, our teacher marked him present. Then she put on sunglasses. Worst joke ever. Best day ever.
